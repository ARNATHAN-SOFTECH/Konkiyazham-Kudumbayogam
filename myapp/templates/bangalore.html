BANGALORE

Bengaluru, commonly known as Bangalore, is the capital city of Karnataka and one of India’s leading technology and innovation hubs. Popularly called the “Silicon Valley of India,” the city is home to numerous IT companies, startups, research institutions, and multinational corporations. Bengaluru is known for its pleasant climate, modern infrastructure, vibrant nightlife, and diverse culture that attracts people from across the country. The city also features beautiful parks, historical landmarks, educational institutions, and a thriving business ecosystem. With its blend of urban development, career opportunities, and cultural diversity, Bengaluru has become one of the most dynamic and fast-growing metropolitan cities in India.
