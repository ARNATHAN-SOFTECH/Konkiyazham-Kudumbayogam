KOTTIYAM

Kottiyam is a growing town located in the Kollam district of Kerala, known for its peaceful residential atmosphere and excellent connectivity. Situated along National Highway 66 between Kollam and Thiruvananthapuram, Kottiyam has developed into an important suburban area with educational institutions, healthcare facilities, local businesses, and transportation access. The town is surrounded by lush greenery, traditional Kerala landscapes, and a welcoming community that reflects the cultural richness of the region. Kottiyam is also recognized for its balance between urban convenience and natural beauty, making it an attractive place for families, students, and businesses alike.
