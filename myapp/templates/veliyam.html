VELIYAM

Veliyam is a serene village located in the Kollam district of Kerala, known for its lush greenery, rural charm, and strong cultural traditions. Surrounded by coconut groves, paddy fields, and gentle landscapes, Veliyam offers a peaceful environment that reflects the natural beauty of Kerala. The village is home to friendly communities, traditional temples, local markets, and educational institutions that contribute to its simple yet vibrant lifestyle. Despite maintaining its rural character, Veliyam has good road connectivity to nearby towns and cities, making it a calm and comfortable place for both residents and visitors.
