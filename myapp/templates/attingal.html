ATTINGAL

Attingal is a historically significant town located in the Thiruvananthapuram district of Kerala, known for its rich cultural heritage, vibrant local economy, and strategic location. Situated along National Highway 66, Attingal serves as an important commercial and transportation hub connecting Thiruvananthapuram with northern parts of the state. The town is famous for its historical importance, including its association with the former Attingal Kingdom and the notable Attingal Revolt, one of the earliest uprisings against colonial rule in India. Surrounded by greenery and traditional Kerala landscapes, Attingal blends modern development with cultural traditions through its markets, temples, churches, educational institutions, and thriving community life.
