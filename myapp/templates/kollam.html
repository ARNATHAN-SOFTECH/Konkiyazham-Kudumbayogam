KOLLAM

Kollam is one of the oldest and most historically significant cities in Kerala, located along the scenic Arabian Sea coast. Known as the gateway to the famous backwaters of Kerala, Kollam is celebrated for its natural beauty, cashew industry, coir production, and rich cultural heritage. The city is home to the picturesque Ashtamudi Lake, which attracts tourists with its tranquil waterways and houseboat experiences. With a blend of tradition and modern development, Kollam features vibrant markets, educational institutions, historic temples, churches, and beaches that reflect the diverse culture of the region. Its strategic location, strong trade history, and peaceful environment make Kollam an important commercial and tourist destination in southern Kerala.
