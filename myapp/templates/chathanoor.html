CHATHANNOOR

Chathannoor is a beautiful and rapidly developing town located in the Kollam district of Kerala. Positioned along the busy National Highway 66, it acts as an important gateway connecting Kollam and Thiruvananthapuram. The town is well known for its greenery, peaceful surroundings, traditional Kerala culture, and friendly community. Chathannoor features a mix of modern development and rural charm, with educational institutions, local businesses, temples, churches, and scenic landscapes contributing to its identity. The area is also appreciated for its accessibility, growing infrastructure, and close connection to Kerala’s rich cultural heritage and natural beauty.
